Privacy

This page covers tapeit.dev and the Tape waitlist. Tape is made by [Company name]. Questions about this page: [contact].

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we store:

  • your email address,
  • when you joined,
  • which of the two forms on the page you used,
  • your answer to “What would you tape first?”, if you picked one,
  • which version of the text next to the form you saw.

That’s all. We don’t store your IP address. To stop automated sign-ups, we count sign-ups per connection per day under a scrambled key that changes daily and is deleted within two days.

Why

To email you about Tape and your invite: one email right after you join, saying you’re on the list, and later your invite. Your answer to “What would you tape first?” helps us decide who to invite first. If you reply to our email, we read your reply to learn what to build. No newsletter, and we never sell or share your address for marketing.

Who processes it

Cloudflare hosts this website and stores the waitlist in its D1 database. Resend sends our emails. Both handle your data only on our behalf.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or tracking pixels. Its fonts are served from this site, and our emails contain no tracking.

Deleting your data

Email hello@tapeit.dev, or reply to any email from us, and we’ll delete your address and anything stored with it. You can also ask us to stop emailing you at any time.

Children

Tape isn’t for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.
